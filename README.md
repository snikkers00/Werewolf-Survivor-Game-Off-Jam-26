**Werewolf Survivors**

Gather and research during the day, defend yourself by night.

Gather. Upgrade. Survive. Repeat...

**Controls:**
- WASD or Arrow Keys to move
- E or Space to interact
- Esc to Pause

In this survivor like you are cursed to live through each night in a cursed werewolf form. Use the precious time you have during the day to prepare yourself for the transformation and the creatures it draws.

*SHIFT* - Game Jam Theme
Shift forms. Shift between day and night. Shift between passive daytime routines and action oriented night time assaults.

Current Features:

- Harvest your bases resources during the day to upgrade your humble home base.
- Spend time at the base during the day to research your affliction and control (or embrace it)
- Level up and upgrade your capabilities in both forms

Planned Features (cut for the Jam):

- Meta Progression
- Advanced Base Building with Defences and Towers
- Crafting Talismans with Advanced Materials for more advanced buffs
- More maps and Characters
